Sunday 9 August · Day trip · Three of us

GIMLI

Eighty-five kilometres north of the city there is a fishing town with an Icelandic accent and the best sand in Manitoba. We have one day.

  • 23°C and sunny
  • 1h 10m drive, Hwy 8 N
  • Sunset 9:00 PM
  • Water warm, shallow entry

The day

Park once near the harbour and walk the rest.

9:00 AM

Leave WinnipegNow

North on Highway 8. Roughly 85 km, an hour and ten minutes with no stops.

10:15 AM

Park at the harbour

Free lots off 1st Avenue. Arrive before noon and you'll still find a spot on a Sunday.

10:20 AM

Coffee at Flatland

41 Centre St · opens 7 AM

Local roaster with a patio. Get a breakfast bagel in you before the sand.

10:45 AM

Seawall and the Viking

Viking Park, 5 min walk south

The harbour wall is painted with the town's history. The statue is the photo everyone takes, and the plaques around it are worth the two minutes.

11:15 AM

Beach

Gimli Beach · public volleyball net

Kilometres of soft sand and a shallow entry, so the water is actually swimmable. Claim a spot near the north end where it thins out.

1:30 PM

Lunch — Beach Boy

70 1st Ave · open till 7 PM

Order the pickerel dinner. Huge plates, cheap, and the enclosed patio looks at the lake.

Swap → Interlake Brewing, two doors down, if you want a beer with the view
2:30 PM

New Iceland Heritage Museum

94 1st Ave · Sundays 1–4 PM only

Half an hour, air conditioned, and it explains why there's a Viking on a Manitoba beach. Don't push this later, it closes at four.

3:15 PM

Sugar Me Cookie Boutique

41 Centre St · closes 6 PM

Butter tarts are the thing here. Grab some for the drive home too.

3:45 PM

Back to the water

Second swim, then dry off on the pier as the light goes gold around six.

5:45 PM

Dinner — Brennivins Pizza Hus

70 1st Ave · opens 4 PM

Unusual pizza menu and good wings, right on the strip.

Swap → Ship & Plough for a pub dinner, live music some nights
7:30 PM

Head home

Optional: stop at the Winnipeg Beach boardwalk, 25 minutes south, for a walk and a food truck before the last stretch.

Good to know

Route
Main St to Highway 8 north is the fast way. Coming home you can drop down Highway 9 through Lockport instead — Half Moon Drive In is right at the bridge.
Parking
Free lots by the harbour and along 1st Ave. It fills up by early afternoon on a sunny Sunday.
Timing
The museum is the only hard deadline: 1–4 PM. Everything else can slide.
Festival
The Icelandic Festival ended on the 3rd, so the town will be busy but not packed.
Sunset
Around 9:00 PM. Gimli faces east, so the light on the pier goes gold rather than giving you a sunset over the water.
